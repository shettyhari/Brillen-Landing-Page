The Delivery System

One brand standard, adapted to the requirements of each site.

01

Survey & brief

Validate site conditions and the brand's operating requirements.

02

Scope & Approvals

Freeze drawings, BOQ, specifications and the execution schedule.

03

Factory + Site Work

Prepare modular elements while site teams progress civil and services work.

04

Install & Handover

Coordinate installation, inspect finishes and close the snag list.

Control Across Every Location

A Named Project Owner

02

04

A Documented Handover
